About BusinessMen.com.ng

Nigeria's fastest-growing business directory. We connect people with verified companies and help businesses establish a professional online presence.

Our Mission

BusinessMen.com.ng was built to solve a simple problem: finding trustworthy businesses in Nigeria should not be difficult. Whether you need a hotel in Lagos, a hospital in Abuja, a school in Port Harcourt, or a supplier in Kano, we bring verified contact information to your fingertips.

We aggregate listings from across Nigeria — covering hotels, hospitals, schools, agriculture, transportation, shopping, real estate, construction, automobile, food, and more — and make them searchable in one place. Every listing includes direct phone numbers, email addresses, and websites so you can reach businesses immediately.

What We Offer

  • Business Directory — Search 86,000+ verified Nigerian businesses by category, city, or keyword. Find phone numbers, emails, and websites instantly.
  • Web Design Services — Professional, affordable websites for Nigerian businesses. From simple informational sites to full e-commerce stores.
  • Free Business Listings — Add your business to our directory at no cost. Reach customers across Nigeria actively searching for your services.
  • Marketplace — Post free ads for products, services, jobs, and more. No account needed.

Our Values

  • Accuracy — We verify business information and allow owners to claim and update their listings directly.
  • Accessibility — Our platform is free to use and works on every device. Finding a business should not cost you anything.
  • Local Focus — We are built for Nigeria, by Nigerians. Every city, every state, every business matters.
  • Transparency — Contact details are displayed openly. No gatekeeping, no forms to fill before seeing a phone number.
