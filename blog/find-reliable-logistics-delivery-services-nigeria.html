How to Find Reliable Logistics and Delivery Services

By BusinessMen Editorial 6 min read Updated May 20, 2026

If you sell products in Nigeria, delivery is your lifeline. Your customer might love your product, but if it arrives late, damaged, or not at all, they'll never buy from you again. And they'll tell their friends not to buy either.

Nigeria's logistics sector has grown enormously in recent years. From established courier companies to tech-enabled delivery startups, you have more options than ever. But more options also mean more confusion about who to trust with your packages.

Types of Delivery Services in Nigeria

Same-Day Delivery

These companies deliver within the same city on the same day. They're perfect for businesses in Lagos, Abuja, or Port Harcourt that need to get products to customers quickly. Prices typically range from ₦1,500 to ₦5,000 per delivery depending on distance and package size.

Interstate Delivery

For sending packages between cities. Delivery times range from 1-5 days depending on the route. Lagos to Abuja might take 1-2 days, while Lagos to Maiduguri could take 3-5 days. Prices range from ₦3,000 to ₦15,000+ depending on weight and distance.

Last-Mile Delivery

These companies specialize in the final leg of delivery from a warehouse or hub to the customer's door. Many e-commerce businesses partner with last-mile delivery companies to handle their customer deliveries.

Freight and Haulage

For bulk shipments and large cargo. If you're moving pallets of goods from a manufacturer in Lagos to distributors across the country, you need a freight company, not a courier service.

How to Choose a Delivery Partner

1. Check Their Coverage Area

Not all delivery companies cover all areas. Some focus on major cities, while others have nationwide networks. Make sure the company delivers to the areas where your customers are located.

If you sell to customers in rural areas, you'll need a company with extensive reach. If you only sell within Lagos, a local same-day delivery service might be more cost-effective.

2. Compare Pricing

Get pricing from multiple companies. But don't just compare the base rate. Look at:

  • Weight and size limits
  • Additional charges for remote areas
  • Insurance coverage for lost or damaged items
  • Return delivery charges (for failed deliveries)
  • Volume discounts if you ship regularly

Some companies offer flat rates within a city, while others charge by distance. Calculate what works best for your typical delivery patterns.

3. Test Their Reliability

Before committing to a delivery partner, send a few test packages. Track them. See how long they actually take. Check the condition of the package on arrival. Call customer service with a question and see how they respond.

A delivery company might promise 24-hour delivery but consistently take 48 hours. Testing reveals the reality behind the promises.

4. Evaluate Their Technology

Modern delivery companies offer:

  • Real-time tracking for you and your customers
  • Automated SMS or email notifications
  • API integration with your e-commerce platform
  • Digital proof of delivery (photos, signatures)
  • Dashboard for managing and analyzing deliveries

These features save you time and give your customers confidence. A company still using paper-based tracking in 2026 might not be the best partner.

5. Check Their Claims Process

Things go wrong. Packages get lost or damaged. What matters is how the company handles it. Ask about their claims process: How long does it take? What documentation do you need? Do they actually pay claims?

Read reviews specifically about how the company handles problems. A company that handles issues well is more valuable than one that never has issues.

6. Assess Their Customer Service

When a delivery is delayed or a customer complains, you need a delivery company that responds quickly. Test their customer service before signing up. Call them. Send an email. Use their WhatsApp line. See how fast and how helpful they are.

Red Flags to Avoid

  • No tracking system: If you can't track your packages, you're flying blind.
  • Unrealistic delivery promises: "We deliver anywhere in Nigeria in 24 hours" is almost certainly false.
  • No insurance option: Your packages should be insured against loss and damage.
  • Poor online reviews: Consistent complaints about lost packages or terrible customer service are deal-breakers.
  • No physical office: A legitimate logistics company has a verifiable location.

Popular Logistics Companies in Nigeria

Some well-known logistics and delivery companies operating in Nigeria include GIG Logistics, DHL Nigeria, FedEx Nigeria, Kwik Delivery, Sendbox, and Red Star Express. Each has different strengths, coverage areas, and pricing structures. Research and compare before choosing.

Final Thoughts

Your delivery partner is an extension of your business. When a customer receives a package, their experience with the delivery company shapes their perception of your brand. Choose a partner that reflects the quality and professionalism you want your customers to associate with your business.

Test multiple companies. Start with small volumes. Scale up with the one that performs best. And always have a backup delivery partner in case your primary one has issues.
