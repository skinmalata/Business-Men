15 Essential Questions to Ask Before Hiring a Moving Company

By BusinessMen Editorial 5 min read Updated May 20, 2026

Moving is stressful enough without having to worry about whether the people handling your belongings are trustworthy. In Nigeria, where the moving industry is largely unregulated, choosing the wrong company can mean broken furniture, stolen items, or a truck that never shows up on moving day.

Here are 15 questions you must ask before hiring any moving company in Nigeria.

Questions About the Company

1. Are you registered with the CAC?

A legitimate moving company should be registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission. Ask for their RC number and verify it on the CAC portal. An unregistered company has no legal accountability.

2. How long have you been in business?

Experience matters in moving. A company that's been operating for 5+ years has likely handled various types of moves and learned from their mistakes. A brand-new company isn't necessarily bad, but you should be more cautious.

3. Can you provide references from recent moves?

Ask for at least three references from customers who moved in the last 6 months. Call them. Ask about punctuality, care of belongings, professionalism, and whether the final cost matched the quote.

4. Do you have insurance coverage for my belongings?

This is critical. If your 65-inch TV gets shattered during the move, who pays for it? A professional moving company should have goods-in-transit insurance. Ask for details about coverage limits and the claims process.

Questions About the Move

5. Will you conduct a pre-move survey?

A reputable company will want to see your belongings before giving you a quote. This can be done in person or via video call. Companies that quote without seeing what they're moving are either guessing (and will likely add costs later) or deliberately underquoting to win your business.

6. What is included in the quoted price?

Get a detailed breakdown: packing materials, labor, transportation, unpacking, assembly/disassembly of furniture. Make sure there are no hidden costs. Ask specifically about additional charges for stairs, long carries, or heavy items.

7. How do you handle fragile and valuable items?

Ask about their packing methods for electronics, glass items, artwork, and antiques. Do they use bubble wrap, specialized boxes, and padding? Will they transport your valuables separately?

8. What happens if there's a delay?

Lagos traffic, vehicle breakdowns, and weather can cause delays. Ask about their policy if the move takes longer than expected. Will they provide temporary storage? Who bears the cost?

9. How many movers will be assigned to my move?

The number of movers affects both speed and safety. A 3-bedroom apartment typically needs 4-6 movers. Fewer than that increases the risk of damage and injury.

10. What type of truck will be used?

Is it a covered truck or an open pickup? Covered trucks protect your belongings from rain and sun. Is the truck in good condition? You don't want your furniture bouncing around in a dilapidated vehicle.

Questions About Payment and Policies

11. What is your payment structure?

Some companies require full payment upfront. Others split it (deposit before the move, balance on delivery). Never pay 100% before the move is completed. A reasonable structure is 30-50% upfront and the rest upon delivery.

12. Is there a cancellation policy?

Plans change. If you need to cancel or reschedule, what are the penalties? Get this in writing.

13. Do you provide packing services?

If you want the company to pack for you, ask about their packing service costs and materials. Some companies include basic packing in their quote, while others charge extra.

14. What is your claims process for damaged items?

Even the best movers occasionally damage something. Ask how they handle damage claims. What documentation do you need? How long does the claims process take?

15. Can I see a written contract before signing?

Never agree to a move without a written contract. The contract should include: the quoted price, services included, moving date and time, payment terms, insurance coverage, and liability for damages.

Red Flags to Watch For

  • No physical address or office: Legitimate companies have a location you can visit.
  • Refusal to provide a written quote: Verbal quotes are meaningless and can change on moving day.
  • Demanding full payment upfront: This is a common scam tactic.
  • Unusually low prices: If a quote is significantly lower than others, there's probably a catch.
  • No insurance: Moving without insurance is unacceptable for a professional company.
  • Generic email addresses: A company using @gmail.com instead of a domain email isn't necessarily a scam, but it's a sign of unprofessionalism.

Final Tips

Get quotes from at least three moving companies. Compare not just prices but services, insurance coverage, and professionalism. Read online reviews. And trust your instincts. If something feels off during your interactions with a company, go with someone else.

Moving is a big investment. Taking the time to choose the right company will save you money, stress, and potentially the loss of your most valuable possessions.
