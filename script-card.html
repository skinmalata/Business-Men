📞 Call Script Card

Business verification cold call → web design pitch

STEP 1 — Opener (15 sec)
SAY
"Hi, I'm Tochukwu from BusinessMen.com.ng.
Your business <business name> is listed on our directory but showing as unverified.
Just calling to confirm you're still operating so I can mark it Verified."
STEP 2 — Qualify (10 sec)
ASK
"Quick question — do customers ever ask if you have a website?"
💡 If YES → proceed to Step 3. If NO → "Would it help if more customers could find you online without having to call first?"
STEP 3 — Pitch (20 sec)
SAY
"We have an in-house web design team that can build you a standard website —
zero upfront payment. We build everything, you review it, and you only pay if you're satisfied.
No deposit, no risk."
💡 Pause after "no risk." Let them speak first.
STEP 4 — Close (15 sec)
SAY
"Can I send you a sample of what a business website in your industry looks like?
If you like it, we can have yours ready in 3–5 days."
💡 Get WhatsApp or email. Send sample immediately. Follow up in 24hrs.
Objection Handling
"I don't have money for that"
"No deposit needed. We build first, you pay only when you're happy. That's how confident we are you'll love it."
"I'll think about it"
"I understand. Let me send you a sample website link so you can see what it looks like — then you decide. No pressure."
"I already have someone"
"No problem. Just so you know, we build websites for free upfront and you only pay at the end. If your current person falls through, I'm a call away."
"I don't need a website"
"Fair enough. Most of your competitors are getting found online every day. Would you at least like to see what they're doing so you know what you're missing?"
"How much does it cost?"
"Standard website starts at ₦150,000. But remember — you don't pay a kobo until you see it and approve it. No deposit."
"Send me an email"
"Sure — what's your WhatsApp? I'll send it there so you can see it immediately. Email works too if you prefer."
Pro Tips
  • ✅ Say their business name — immediately proves you're not a random caller
  • ✅ Pause often — the first person to speak after the pitch usually loses
  • ✅ Never ask "Do you want a website?" — ask "Do customers ask if you have one?"
  • ✅ Use "verify" not "sell" — you're doing them a favour first
  • ✅ Send sample immediately — strike while the iron is warm
  • ✅ If they say no, thank them and mark the business verified anyway — goodwill goes far
