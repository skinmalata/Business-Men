Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily submit, such as business names, contact details, and marketplace ad content when you use our forms. We may also collect basic usage data (pages visited, browser type) via cookies and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use submitted information to:

  • Display business listings on the directory
  • Post marketplace ads as requested
  • Improve our site and user experience
  • Respond to inquiries via our contact form

3. Information Sharing

Business listings and marketplace ads are public by nature and visible to all site visitors. We do not sell personal information to third parties. We may share data with service providers (e.g., Firebase, ImgBB) necessary to operate the site.

4. Data Storage

Data is stored securely on Google Firebase (Firestore) servers. Marketplace ads are automatically deleted after expiration (48 hours). Business listings may remain until removed by request.

5. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for site functionality. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

This site uses:

  • Firebase (Google) — database and hosting backend
  • ImgBB — image hosting for marketplace photos
  • GitHub Pages — site hosting

Each service has its own privacy policy governing data handling.

7. Your Rights

You may request removal of your business listing or personal data by contacting us. We will respond within 14 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
